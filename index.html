The Artist

Aurora Montelongo

Aurora Montelongo's work lives at the intersection of architecture, nature, and the human gaze. With an editorial eye refined through years of practice, she captures spaces and subjects with a quiet intensity that resonates with collectors, interior designers, and those drawn to visual atmosphere.

Her photographs have found a place in private residences, design studios, and curated exhibitions, valued not merely as images, but as compositional works meant to inhabit a room.

Grounded in a deep sensitivity to light, structure, and narrative, Aurora's portfolio moves between the monumental and the intimate, from sweeping landscapes and architectural studies to striking portraiture.

— A. Montelongo
